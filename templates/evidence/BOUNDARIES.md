# Evidence boundaries

The limits on what your materials may say. The assistant reads this together with `claims.json` before
drafting or reviewing anything. Add a line whenever you find a way a statement could overreach.

## Always true

- A resume, letter, answer or message is never evidence. Only the ledger is.
- Team credit is not personal credit. "The team built X" never becomes "I built X".
- A tool the team used is not a tool you used, unless the ledger says so.
- Numbers keep their scope: what was counted, over what period, against what baseline, measured by whom.
- A prototype is not production. A pilot is not adoption. A public site is not proof of users.
- Coursework and training are not professional experience. Certifications only if held, with their exact names.
- Confidential employer or client material (internal names, unreleased products, non-public metrics) stays out
  unless you confirm it is public or approved.
- Motives, lessons, conversations and personal stories come only from what you said, recorded in your words.
